About the institute

A technical campus built around labs, not lecture halls alone

KIET was set up to train engineers who can work from day one — every department runs its theory alongside dedicated labs, project rooms, and industry-linked coursework, rather than treating practicals as an afterthought.

Students split their time between core classes, lab sessions, and project work with faculty mentors, building a portfolio of applied work well before their final placement season.

The campus in Muradnagar houses classrooms, laboratories, a central library, hostels, and sports facilities within walking distance of each other, so a student's day runs across one connected site rather than several.

Campus life

Everything a student needs within one campus

Academic facilities sit alongside residential and recreational spaces, so students rarely need to leave the campus during the week.

Central library

Reading halls, technical journals, and a digital repository open through the evening for project research.

Computing & engineering labs

Department-specific labs for programming, electronics, mechanics, and pharmacy practicals.

Residential hostels

Separate hostels for men and women, with mess facilities and wardens on site.

Sports & recreation

Grounds and courts for cricket, football, basketball, and athletics alongside an indoor games room.

Placement cell

Resume reviews, mock interviews, and coordination with recruiting companies from the third year onward.

An on-campus health centre with a resident nurse and visiting doctor for routine care.
